RULE: only Hưng Thành Phát's own photographs go in this folder.

Never a supplier's catalogue image, never a competitor's product shot, never a
screenshot of another door company's website. The site tells visitors "ảnh thực
tế tại công trình, không phải ảnh mẫu" — that promise has to hold everywhere.

(In Aug 2026 this folder was cleared of ~20 images taken from a competitor's
catalogue. Several still carried their watermark and brand name, and two were
rendering on the homepage hero.)

What lives here now
-------------------
Real cases/   Photos from HTP's own Facebook page. Used by GALLERY_IMAGES in
              lib/site-data.ts.

Where product photos come from now
----------------------------------
Real installs live in public/cong-trinh/<slug>/ and are indexed in
lib/cong-trinh-data.ts (generated by Enki/htp/cong-trinh/export_to_site.py).
The product catalog pulls from there via `showcase` in PRODUCT_CATEGORIES, so
every product card is a job HTP actually did.

Still missing
-------------
Motor, bình lưu điện, remote and còi báo động have no HTP photos at all, so that
category renders as text tiles instead of cards. To give it cards: photograph the
units the xưởng actually stocks (plain background, good light, ~1600px wide), drop
them in public/products/motor-binh-luu-dien/, and fill the `image` fields back in
on that category in lib/site-data.ts.
